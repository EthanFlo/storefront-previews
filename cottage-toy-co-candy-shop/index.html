Our story

A small Main Street shop, opened by Lisa.

Lisa Cellucci opened Cottage Toy Co. & Candy Shop on Main Street in Manchester Center in late 2025. The idea was simple. A toy and candy shop in the middle of town, the kind of place a kid pulls a parent into and a parent doesn't mind walking into.

Everything in here is picked by hand. If Lisa wouldn't give it to a kid she knows, it doesn't go on the shelf. Stop in, say hello, and let the little ones take their time choosing.
