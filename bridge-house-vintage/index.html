Owner-run on Warren Street

Judy keeps the floor changing, so it's worth coming back.

Bridge House Vintage is run by Judy Fertel right on Warren Street, the stretch of Hudson known for antiques and design. The shop is small on purpose. Everything on the floor was picked by hand, set out with care, and priced to actually go home with someone.

You'll find vintage and mid-century furniture, lighting, art, home decor, and curated antiques. Stock turns over often, so the pieces you see today may be gone next week and replaced with something new.

What's on the floor

The collection

Categories change with what comes in. Here's the kind of thing you can expect to find when you walk through the door.

  • 01

    Vintage & mid-century furniture

    Credenzas, dining chairs, sideboards, lounge seating and case pieces with the lines and wood that hold up decade after decade.

  • 02

    Lighting, art & decor

    Table and floor lamps, framed art, mirrors and the small things that finish a room and give it a point of view.

  • 03

    Curated antiques

    Older pieces chosen for character and condition, sitting comfortably next to the mid-century stock.

  • 04

    One-of-a-kind finds

    The pieces with no category. If you love it, take it home, because it won't be here long.

How the shop works

Come in, take your time, ask questions.

The best way to know what's here is to stop by. Pieces are arranged so you can see them in a room rather than stacked in a pile. If something catches your eye, ask. Judy knows where it came from and what it is.

  • Browse the current furniture, lighting and art in person.
  • Ask about a piece you saw on Instagram or Chairish.
  • Call ahead to check a piece is still available.
