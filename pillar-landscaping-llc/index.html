What we do

From clearing the lot to the finished patio

We take on the dirt work and the detail work. That means we can clear a site, shape the grade, and come back to build the stonework, so you're not chasing three different contractors.

  • Excavation

    Digging, grading, and shaping ground for new construction, additions, and yard projects.

  • Land clearing

    Clearing brush and trees and prepping raw land, including site prep for building.

  • Stump removal

    Pulling and grinding out stumps so you can reclaim the space or build over it clean.

  • Dirt driveways

    Cutting in and grading dirt and gravel driveways that hold up to New England seasons.

  • Lawn expansions

    Opening up your usable yard, reshaping the grade, and seeding new lawn areas.

  • Patios & walls

    Stone patios, retaining walls, and general residential hardscaping and landscaping.
