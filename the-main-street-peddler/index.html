The Shop

A Main Street shop made for slow browsing

The Main Street Peddler sits on East Main Street in historic downtown Lititz. It is owner-operated by Chris Sensenich, so the person who picks the pieces is usually the person who says hello at the door.

The shelves lean cottage and garden. Soft, lived-in pieces for the home and the porch, the kind of things you set down and then quietly love for years. Stop in, take your time, and ask Chris anything.

What You'll Find

A few of the things on our shelves

The mix changes through the seasons, so this is a taste rather than a full list. Call ahead if you are hunting for something specific and Chris will let you know what is in.

🏠

Cottage and garden decor

Soft, homey pieces for the kitchen, the mantel, and the porch. The cottage look, done warmly.

Nora Fleming

The serving platters and the little seasonal minis that snap on. A nice gift that people actually use.

Collectibles and gifts

Small finds and giftable pieces for birthdays, hostesses, and the people who are hard to shop for.
