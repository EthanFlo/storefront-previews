On Main Street

A small shop in the middle of one of Georgia's prettiest downtowns.

Madison's historic Main Street is the kind of place people drive in to walk around. Laughing Moon is part of that walk. You pop in, you browse, and more often than not you leave holding something you weren't looking for.

It's a local favorite that mostly lived on Facebook and Instagram until now. This page is here so people can actually find you, see what you carry, and know how to get to the door.
