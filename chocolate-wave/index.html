Carmen & Dan

Two friends, one small kitchen, a lot of chocolate.

Carmen Kulak and Dan Moldovan have known each other since high school in Romania. They both spent years working in local candy shops, learning how tempering really works and how a caramel should pull. In 2021 they took what they knew and opened their own counter here on First Street.

That's the whole operation. The two of them make everything in the back, fresh, in small batches, with no preservatives. If something's in the case, it's because one of them poured it, dipped it, or painted it by hand earlier that day.

Before you stop by

Good to know

Is everything really made in-store?

Yes. Carmen and Dan make it all in the back of the shop, fresh, in small batches, with no preservatives. If it's in the case, one of them made it that day.

Can I build my own bark?

You can, and the toppings are free. Pick your chocolate, then add as much as you'd like. It's a hit with kids and a quiet weakness for plenty of adults.

What's the Rehoboth Beach bar?

It's a chocolate bar made just for this town, with caramel and marshmallow inside. Made in-house, and you won't find it anywhere else.

Can you box up a gift or a tin to take home?

Happily. Call ahead with the occasion or how much you want, and we'll have it boxed and ready at the counter.

Do you ship or take online orders?

Not yet. For now the way to order is a phone call. We'll tell you honestly what travels well and what's best eaten fresh.

Are you open off season?

Yes, we're open year round. Summer is the busy stretch, so for the quieter months a quick call confirms the hours that day.
