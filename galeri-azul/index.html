On the plaza

Right in the middle of old Mesilla

Mesilla is one of those towns people slow down for. Adobe walls, the old plaza, and shops you actually want to wander through. Galeri Azul sits on Calle de Guadalupe, a short walk from the square.

Inside it's color in every corner and nothing quite like it. People fall for a piece all the time. The catch is, until now the only way to find us again was Facebook. This page fixes that. Now you can see what we're about, get the address, and call before you drive over.
