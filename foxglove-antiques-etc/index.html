What you'll find inside

A shop built around pieces that last.

Foxglove keeps a real range under one roof. Here's the heart of what Karen carries, and what tends to move through the floor.

01

American, English & Continental antiques

Pieces with a history on both sides of the Atlantic. The kind collectors keep an eye out for, side by side on one floor.

02

Oil paintings & porcelain

Framed oils and fine porcelain for the wall and the cabinet. Worth coming in to see in person, since photos never quite do them justice.

03

Antique furniture

Tables, chests, casegoods, and seating with real age and real wood. Built to be used, not just looked at.

The shop

On West Main Street, in the middle of Abingdon.

Abingdon is a town people come to wander. West Main Street has the brick sidewalks, the old storefronts, and the kind of afternoon where you walk in for one thing and leave with another.

Foxglove sits right in it. Inside, the stock shifts as pieces come and go, so two visits rarely look the same. That's part of the fun. If you're planning a trip through Abingdon, the shop is an easy stop, and an easy place to lose track of time.

Karen runs it the old way. Real pieces, fair conversation, no rush. Call ahead if you want to be sure something's still here, and we'll go look for you.
