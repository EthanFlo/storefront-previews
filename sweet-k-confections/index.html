The signature

The brigadeiro is why people come back.

A brigadeiro is a small Brazilian chocolate sweet, rolled by hand and finished with sprinkles. Karla makes them the way she grew up making them, and they have a way of disappearing first at every party.

The custom cakes are the centerpiece. The brigadeiros and dulce de leche fillings are the part guests text you about the next day.

The baker

Hi, I'm Karla.

I'm a self-taught baker, and I grew up baking with my family in Brazil. That's where the brigadeiros and the dulce de leche come from. They're not an add-on for me. They're home.

Sweet K Confections is my own studio. I take a small number of orders so I can stay hands-on with each one, from the first conversation about your event to the cake on the table. If you want something detailed and a little personal, that's the part I love most.
