The shop

One room, a lot of stories.

Walk in off William Street and you will find shelves that change all the time. A ship's lantern next to a tray of estate rings. A carved icon beside a stack of old maps. Leslie and Wes run the place themselves, so the things on the shelves are the things they went out and found.

Nothing here is mass produced. Most pieces are one of one, which means the shop on a Tuesday rarely looks like the shop the week before. If you saw something you liked, it helps to come back soon or give us a call to hold it.

What you'll find

A cabinet of curiosities

Four things we tend to have, though the particular pieces are always changing. Call ahead if you are hunting for something specific and we will tell you what's on the shelf.

01

Nautical pieces

The heart of the shop, fitting for the Eastern Shore. Ship's lanterns, brass instruments, old charts, buoys, and the odd thing that came off a boat and found its way to a shelf.

02

Antiques, art and artifacts

Furniture, paintings, prints, and small artifacts with some age on them. The kind of object that has clearly lived somewhere before it got to us.

03

Jewelry

Estate and vintage jewelry, from quiet everyday rings to pieces with a bit more drama. Worth a slow look through the case.

04

Religious art and Orthodox icons

A small, considered selection of religious art, including Orthodox icons. People come from a fair distance for these, so it is worth a call to ask what we have in.

We buy what we love and hope someone else loves it too. That's about the whole business plan.

Leslie and Wes, Coconut Bay Trading Company
