In Lynn Valley

A small counter that
knows its regulars

Tucked inside Lynn Valley Centre, Sushi5 is the kind of neighbourhood spot you end up at once a week without quite deciding to. The nigiri and sashimi come out fresh, the tempura crisp and never greasy, and the lunch combos let you mix and match until the plate is exactly yours.

Service is quick and kind, even when the room is full. The staff greet the people they know, the broth gets a reputation, and the value keeps the seats warm. No fuss, no theatre, just good Japanese food made the same whether you eat in or take it home.
