335 High Street

On Main Street, since 2024.

Jerry Moe's is a newer face on High Street, the main downtown shopping block in Chestertown. The district goes back to 1706, and it's the kind of walkable Eastern Shore street where you can park once and get the whole afternoon done on foot.

It's owner run, the owner is around, and she's part of the town. Come in and you'll get help from someone who knows the shop and the regulars, not a call center.

  • 7 Days a week. Open every day, so a Sunday fitting works as well as a Tuesday.
