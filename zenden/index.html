What you will find inside

A shop that feels like a deep breath.

Susan opened ZenDen to give the Lowcountry a quiet, earthy place to slow down and pick out something meaningful. Step in and you will find shelves of crystals, bundles of sage, incense and essential oils, and handmade jewelry made close to home. Most of what we carry comes from people who live and work right here.

If you are new to any of it, just ask. We are happy to talk you through which stones do what, how to burn sage, or which piece might suit the person you are shopping for.

More than twenty-five local makers

Shelves stocked by your neighbors.

ZenDen represents more than twenty-five local artists and artisans. That means the jewelry, art, and small goods you find here were made by people in this community, not pulled off a warehouse pallet. When you buy something at ZenDen, you are supporting a maker down the road.

The selection shifts as artists bring in new work, so it is worth stopping by often. There is usually something new on the shelf.

Classes & private sessions

The Healing House

Beyond the shop floor, ZenDen runs The Healing House: a space for classes and private sessions. It is where the boutique becomes something you take part in, not just shop from. Call to hear what is on the calendar and to book a spot or a private session.

  • Classes

    Group sessions in our quiet back space. Dates change, so call ahead for the current schedule.

  • Private sessions

    One-on-one time booked at a pace that suits you. Reach out and we will find a time.

  • How to join

    The simplest way in is a phone call. We will walk you through what is open and what to expect.
