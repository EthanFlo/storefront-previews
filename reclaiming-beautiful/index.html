On Myrtle Street

Run by Monique, in the heart of downtown Stillwater.

Stillwater is a river town, and people come down Myrtle Street to wander. Our door is part of that walk. Folks step in, run a hand along a dresser, ask where it came from, and leave with something they didn't know they needed.

The hard part has always been after. Someone falls for a piece, goes home, and can't find us again online. That's what this site is here to fix. Now there's a place to point people, a number to call, and a way to find the shop again.

Stop in when you're downtown. We'd love to show you around.

Good to know

First questions, answered.

Where are you, and how do I get there?

We're at 216 Myrtle Street W in downtown Stillwater, MN. Myrtle runs right through the middle of downtown, near the river. There's tap-to-open directions below that'll route you straight to the door.

What are your hours?

We've put a sample weekly schedule lower on this page. Please treat it as a placeholder and call ahead at 651-342-0553 to confirm. We'll lock in the real hours with you before this goes live.

Where do I park?

Downtown Stillwater has street parking along Myrtle and the surrounding blocks, plus public lots a short walk away. If you're coming for something large, give us a call and we'll help you sort out the closest spot.

Can I buy online?

Not yet. For now everything sells in person at the shop, which is half the fun. Call us at 651-342-0553 if you want to ask about a piece or have us hold it.

Do you take in furniture to refinish?

That's a conversation we're always happy to have. Give us a call and tell us about the piece, and we'll let you know what's possible.
