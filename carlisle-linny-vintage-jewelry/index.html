The shop on Churton Street

Come in, take your time, find the thing you didn't know you wanted.

Carlisle & Linny sits on South Churton Street in downtown Hillsborough, the part of town where it's easy to spend an afternoon wandering in and out of doorways. This is a place to slow down and actually look. Pick things up. Turn them over.

The cases are full of pieces that someone wore and loved before you. Lindsley keeps the stock moving, so there's almost always something new since the last time you stopped by. That's half the fun of a vintage shop, you never quite know what's going to be sitting there.

If you fell for a piece last week and can't stop thinking about it, the safe move is to call ahead. Things here don't tend to stay long.
