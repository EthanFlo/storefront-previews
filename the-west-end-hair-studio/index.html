The specialty

Extensions done right, then nobody can tell.

Most of what Karen does starts with one question: how do we add length or fullness so it still looks like your hair. She's certified in several extension methods, so the right one gets matched to your hair type and how you live. Good extensions move when you do, sit flat, and grow out cleanly. That's the whole point.

She built a name as a color person too, so a lot of clients come in for balayage, a soft ombre, or a full color change and stay for the cut. Whatever you book, you'll get a real look at your hair first, and an honest plan for it.

Behind the chair

Karen Duddy has been doing this since 1996.

She's a Seacoast stylist with more than twenty years in, and a Certified Master Hair Extension specialist, which is about as far as the extension training goes. Clients come back for her eye on color and the fact that she'll tell you the truth about what will and won't work for your hair.

Karen also donates hair to Children With Hair Loss, a nonprofit that makes wigs for kids going through medical hair loss. Samantha and Riannon round out the team, so there's a chair for you even when Karen's booked.

Before you book

Good to know

How do I book?

You can book online through Vagaro, or call the studio at (603) 373-8947 and we'll find a time. For extensions or Hairskeen, a quick consultation comes first so we get the details right.

What kind of extensions do you do?

Karen is a Certified Master Hair Extension specialist and works with several certified methods. The right one depends on your hair type, your lifestyle, and the result you want. We sort that out together at the consultation.

Do consultations cost anything?

No. The first consultation is complimentary. You'll leave with an honest plan and a price before you commit to anything.

What is Hairskeen?

Hairskeen is a non-surgical hair replacement option for men who want more density without a procedure. It's an uncommon service for the area. Call and Karen will explain how it works for your situation.

Can I see Karen specifically?

Yes, you can request Karen. Samantha and Riannon also take clients, so if Karen's booked out there's still a chair for you sooner.

Where exactly are you?

801 Islington Street, in Portsmouth's West End. There's plenty going on around the block, so people often pair an appointment with lunch nearby.
