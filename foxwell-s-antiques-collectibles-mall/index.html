The building on US-50

A treasure-trove you can lose an afternoon in.

Foxwell’s isn’t a single dusty shop — it’s a climate-controlled, 10,000‑square‑foot mall where more than sixty vetted dealers each keep a booth. That’s why people plan a trip around it: the inventory turns over constantly, and no two visits are the same. You’ll find it in its own building with its own parking lot, right on Ocean Gateway, the main highway through Easton on the way to St. Michaels and the Bay.

For about three decades, Charlie and Glory have kept the doors open seven days a week and the welcome warm — the kind of place where a serious collector and a first‑time browser get the same friendly hello.

Before you drive over

Good to know

Are you open today?

Almost certainly — Foxwell’s is open every day, 10 AM to 5 PM. If you’re coming from a distance, a quick call confirms it.

Where exactly are you on US-50?

7793 Ocean Gateway in Easton — a standalone building with its own parking lot, right on the main highway through town toward St. Michaels and the Bay.

Is there parking?

Yes — free parking in our own lot, with easy in-and-out right off the highway.

Do you buy or take consignments?

We’re a multi-dealer mall, so booths and arrangements vary. Give us a call and we’ll point you to the right person.

Can you hold an item for me?

Often, yes. Call the mall and we’ll do our best to set it aside while you make your way over.

Do you ship?

Ask when you call — shipping depends on the piece and the dealer, and we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.
