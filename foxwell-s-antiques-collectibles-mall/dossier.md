## Why it's a strong lead
- ~30 years in business ("30 years of serving the antique & collectible needs of our valued customers")
- 10,000 sq ft, climate-controlled, 60+ independent dealers under one roof
- Open 7 days a week, 10–5; standalone building with its own parking lot on US-50
- Rated 4.4/5 on TripAdvisor (16 reviews); ranked #7 of 26 things to do in Easton; positive across Yelp, Google, Nextdoor
- On Maryland's affluent Eastern Shore, high antiquing/tourist traffic toward St. Michaels and the Bay

## Signature specialties (used to build the Collections section)
- Quality antique furniture & early American primitives
- Civil War / WWI / WWII militaria
- Chesapeake waterfowl/duck decoys and oyster plates (Eastern Shore specialties)
- Sterling silver, rare books, estate & nautical jewelry
- Mid-century modern, paintings, fine china (Imari, Rose Medallion, Fostoria, Waterford)

## Revenue at risk (the pitch)
Inventory "changes daily" and skews to higher-ticket collector goods, yet there is no searchable
online catalog, no e-commerce, and no email capture. Every out-of-area collector and tourist must
phone or visit in person. A real site with rotating featured finds, photos, and a contact/hold form
would capture trip-planners, remote collectors, and consignor leads now going to competitors with
online listings. Current online identity rests on a personal Yahoo address and social pages.

## "No dedicated website" — honest status
No owned domain. A free auto-generated listing page exists at foxwells-collectibles.edan.io (an EDAN
listing-builder microsite, returned 403) — not a true owned website. Tourism and review listings carry
no website link; only Facebook + YouTube. Confirm with the owner that they don't consider the EDAN page
"their website."
