What you find inside

A floor that changes every week.

Nothing here is mass produced. Stock turns over as Meghan finds and refinishes pieces, so the shop looks a little different each time you walk in. Here is what tends to be on hand.

  • 01

    Retro and vintage lighting

    Table lamps, hanging fixtures, and shades from past decades, cleaned up and ready to plug in at home.

  • 02

    Upcycled, hand-painted furniture

    Dressers, side tables, and case pieces given new color and life by hand, one at a time.

  • 03

    Home decor and accessories

    Mirrors, art, glassware, and small vintage objects to finish a room or fill a shelf.

  • 04

The shop

Run by one person who actually picks every piece.

Vintage Vibe is owner-operated by Meghan Dwyer Pierson, right on State Street in downtown Doylestown. She hunts down the lighting and furniture, does the painting and refinishing, and sets up the floor herself.

That means real answers when you ask about a piece, honest notes on its age and condition, and a shop with a point of view instead of a warehouse of the same five things.

  • Hand finished. Painting and refinishing done in house, not bought in bulk.
  • Ever changing. New finds arrive as fast as old ones sell.
  • Small and local. A State Street shop, not a chain.
