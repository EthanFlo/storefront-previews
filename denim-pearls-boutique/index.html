Why we're here

A Donnelly Street shop for the way Mount Dora actually dresses.

Downtown Mount Dora is the kind of place you stroll. People come for the brick streets and the lake, they wander Donnelly, and they stop in shops that feel personal. We wanted Denim & Pearls to be one of those stops.

The idea was simple. Carry clothes that fit real bodies, from small through 4X, and treat plus sizes like the rest of the store instead of an afterthought. Add the jewelry and accessories that finish a look, and keep it a place where you can ask for help and actually get it.

For now most folks find us on Facebook, which is part of why this page exists. If you've been meaning to stop by, here's everything you need to plan the trip.
