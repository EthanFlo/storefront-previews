Our story

A new shop in a spot Rehoboth already loved.

Happy Place opened in January 2025. It's Heather and Dominick Napolitano's first store, and they're running it together as a husband-and-wife team.

The space has been a gift shop on Rehoboth Avenue for more than twenty years. It was home to M2, a shop a lot of locals knew well. Heather and Dominick took the keys and kept that going, with their own mix of gifts and a friendlier setup for families.

“

The plan is simple. Keep the corner of Rehoboth Avenue that people already came back for, and make it a little more fun to walk into.

There's a kids station, so you can shop.

Bring the little ones. There's a spot set up to keep kids busy while you look around, which makes a real difference when you're trying to find a gift and a five-year-old is ready to leave. It's a small thing that says a lot about who the shop is for.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Where are you, exactly?

146 Rehoboth Avenue, right on the main downtown shopping strip in Rehoboth Beach, a short walk from the boardwalk. Open the map.

How is it different from the other gift shops here?

It isn't the usual T-shirt souvenir shop. The mix runs toward real gifts, home pieces, jewelry, and work from local makers, picked by hand by the owners.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. There's a kids activity station, and sections set up for grandparents, parents, and dogs. Families are the point here.

Can you hold something or help me pick a gift?

Give the shop a call at (302) 569-0295. Heather or Dominick can tell you what's in and help you sort out a gift.

Do you ship or sell online?

Not yet. For now the best way is to stop in or call. Online ordering is something the shop is working toward.
