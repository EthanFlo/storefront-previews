Off the griddle

What we make every morning

The menu changes with the seasons, and the desserts depend on what got baked that day. Here is what regulars come back for. Call ahead if you want to know what's on the board.

Cinnamon rolls

Made from scratch and best while they're still warm. They go fast on a busy weekend, so come early.

Corned beef hash

House-made, browned on the flat-top, the kind you can't get from a can. A regular order with eggs any style.

Pancakes, waffles & omelets

Buttermilk pancakes and golden waffles, poured-to-order omelets, and real maple syrup on the table.

Stuffed French toast

Thick-cut and filled. Blueberry one week, strawberry shortcake the next. Ask what's being stuffed today.

Muffins, pies & desserts

Homemade muffins out of the oven and seasonal pies on the counter. They rotate, so the case is never the same twice.

The house hot sauce

Our award-winning hot sauce. Put it on the hash, put it on the eggs. Regulars ask for it by name.

Made here, by hand

Everything starts from scratch

The hash is mixed in the kitchen. The rolls are rolled by hand. The muffins and pies come out of the oven here, not off a truck. It takes longer and that's the point.

Breakfast runs into lunch, so you can show up hungry late in the morning and still get a full plate. When the weekend rush hits, the line moves and the coffee keeps coming.

Our story

A Wolfeboro fixture for over 35 years

Katie's Kitchen has been part of mornings in Wolfeboro for more than 35 years. Jason Gurnari took over in 2020 and kept the things people count on, the scratch cooking, the regulars at the counter, and the steady griddle that gets going before the town wakes up.

You'll find us in Clarke Plaza on Center Street, in the middle of downtown. Wolfeboro sits on Lake Winnipesaukee and fills up with visitors all summer, so weekends get busy. Locals know to come early. Visitors learn fast.

On the walls

Local art, on rotation

The walls aren't decoration we picked once and forgot. We hang work from local artists and rotate it, so the room looks a little different through the year. Eat your eggs, look up, see something new.

If you're a Wolfeboro-area artist who'd like a wall, ask your server or give us a call. We're glad to talk.

Before you drive over

What are your hours?

We're a breakfast and lunch spot, open mornings into the early afternoon. Hours can shift with the season, so call us at (603) 569-1406 for today's hours before you head out.

Is there usually a wait?

On busy summer weekends, yes, there can be a line. It moves. If you'd rather time it, give us a quick call and we'll tell you how things look.

Where exactly are you?

35 Center Street, in Clarke Plaza, in downtown Wolfeboro. There's parking in the plaza. Open the map.

Do the cinnamon rolls and specials sell out?

Some days, yes. The from-scratch items and daily specials are made in batches, so coming early is the safest bet.
