What you'll find

A working shop, not a museum. Though it reads like one.

The stock turns over the way a good used bookshop should. Some of it is genuinely rare. Some of it is the paperback you've been meaning to reread. Here is the shape of it.

01

Antiquarian & rare books

Older volumes, scarce printings, and books that have earned their wear. Bring a title you're hunting for and Sonny will tell you straight whether it's likely to turn up.

02

First editions

First printings worth holding onto, kept for collectors and for readers who like the original object. Condition is described honestly, point by point.

03

Used books in every field

History, poetry, natural history, fiction, the odd and the practical. The fields run wide, so plan to wander a while.

04

Cards & curiosities

Prints, ephemera, and small finds that don't fit on a shelf. Good for a gift, or for the kind of browser who can't leave empty-handed.

The bookseller

Sonny Saul has been at this a long time.

Pleasant Street Books is owner-operated by Sonny Saul, a longtime antiquarian bookseller in Woodstock. That means the person who priced the book is usually the person standing behind the counter, and the one who can tell you where it came from.

It's the kind of shop where a question about an author turns into a half-hour conversation, and you leave with two books you didn't know you wanted. If you collect, he'll keep an eye out. If you're just browsing, that's the whole point.

  • Looking for a particular title or author? Call and ask.
  • Have books to sell? Worth a conversation before you box them up.
  • Listed with AbeBooks as a dealer, so some stock travels.

A bookshop is one of the few places left where you can lose an afternoon on purpose. This one is on Pleasant Street.
