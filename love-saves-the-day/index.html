The shop

A small shop packed with the good stuff.

Love Saves the Day has held down the corner of Main and Bridge in New Hope for years. It is the kind of place you walk into for one record and leave with a jacket, a movie poster, and a toy you forgot you wanted. Cleo runs it, and every shelf has a story.

The corner

Main and Bridge, in the middle of it all.

New Hope is an artsy river town in Bucks County, and Love Saves the Day sits right on the corner where Main Street meets Bridge. Park, wander the block, and let the shop pull you in.

  • Corner of Main and Bridge. Easy to find, easy to lose an hour in.
  • Owner-operated. Cleo knows the stock and is happy to point you to it.
  • Always changing. New finds come in often, so it pays to come back.
