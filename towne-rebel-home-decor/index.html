On Main Street in Galena

A shop that's easy to lose track of time in.

Galena pulls people in for a slow weekend, and Main Street is where most of it happens. Towne Rebel sits right in the middle of that, at 210 N Main, with the door open and plenty to poke through.

Darlene keeps the shop the way she'd want to shop herself. Things are grouped so you can wander, pick something up, set it back down, and find something you weren't even looking for. No rush, no pressure.

If you're planning a Galena trip, put us on the walk. And if you're local, you already know where to find us.
