In the shop

Things you won't find just anywhere

Every shelf here is something we chose ourselves. Here's a look at what tends to catch people first.

01

Santons de Provence

Hand figurines from Provence, the little clay characters that fill a French nativity scene. Small, detailed, and easy to collect one at a time.

02

Quimper faience

Hand painted French pottery from Brittany. Plates, bowls, and pieces with the soft folk patterns Quimper has been known for since the 1700s.

03

Laguiole knives

The classic French folding knife, with the bee at the spine. Good for the table, good as a gift, and made to be kept for years.

04

Provence tablecloths

Printed cotton linens in the colors of the south of France. They bring a Sunday lunch feeling to a kitchen table back home.

05

Soaps from Provence

French soaps with the scents you'd expect from a market in Provence. Simple, honest, and the kind of small gift people remember.

06

French perfumes

Perfumes carried over from France, plus the gifts and accessories that go alongside them. Come in and we'll show you what's new.

Our story

French finds, on a quiet street in Blowing Rock.

Blowing Rock is a small mountain town, and Morris Street is the kind of street you walk slowly. de Provence et d'ailleurs sits right in it, a little shop with France inside the door.

Danielle brings these pieces back from France herself. The santons, the Quimper pottery, the Laguiole knives, the soaps and linens from Provence. They're chosen one at a time, so what's on the shelf is what we actually loved.

If you've ever come home from a trip wishing you'd bought that one thing, this is the shop for that feeling. Come find the piece you'll be glad you kept.

Good to know

A few first questions

Where are you, exactly?

We're at 131-8 Morris Street in Blowing Rock, NC 28605, right in the middle of town. You can tap Get Directions and your phone will map it for you.

Is there parking nearby?

Morris Street is in walking distance of the main shopping area in Blowing Rock, so most folks park nearby and stroll over. Give us a call and we'll point you to the closest spot on the day.

What kinds of things do you carry?

French gifts and accessories, Santons de Provence figurines, Quimper faience pottery, Laguiole knives, and Provence tablecloths, soaps, and perfumes. Much of it is brought back from France by hand.

What are your hours?

Hours can shift with the season here in the mountains, so the best thing is to call ahead at (828) 295-9989. The hours listed lower on this page are a placeholder for us to confirm with you.

Can you help me find a gift?

Yes, that's the fun part. Call or stop in and tell us who it's for, and we'll walk you to a few things that fit.
